# Link check accuracy — 2026-11-24

Run of `scripts/eval-links.mjs` (the same `inspect()` the app uses). Took 74 s.

**Data**
- Phishing: 77 links from the public OpenPhish community feed on 2026-11-24 (300 links in the feed; one per registrable domain). 61 were still online when checked, 16 were already down or unreachable.
- Legitimate: 177 sites. 107 are on the official UAE list (government, banks, utilities, telcos, airlines, couriers); 70 are other real sites, including shops, news and login pages (`eval/legit-extra.txt`).

**Results** (a link is "flagged" when its link score reaches the threshold)

| Threshold | Caught (recall) | Precision | False alarms on legit sites | F1 | TP | FN | FP | TN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 (suspicious) | 22.1% | 89.5% | 1.1% | 35.4% | 17 | 60 | 2 | 175 |
| 60 (high risk) | 3.9% | 75.0% | 0.6% | 7.4% | 3 | 74 | 1 | 176 |

| Subset | Recall at 30 | Recall at 60 |
|---|---|---|
| Phishing still online (61) | 26.2% | 4.9% |
| Phishing already down (16) | 6.3% | 0.0% |

False alarms: official UAE sites 0 of 107; other legitimate sites 2 of 70.

**How often each signal fired on phishing links**: new domain (<30 days) 0, free hosting platform 15, page asks for card/password/code 6, copies a known brand 9, redirect or short link 6, look-alike or risky name 16.

**Legitimate sites flagged (score 30+)**

- https://github.com/login — 30: The page asks for a password and is not an official site
- https://www.netflix.com/login — 75: The page asks for a password and is not an official site; The page uses the name Google but is not its official site

**Limits.** OpenPhish is global phishing (mostly fake login pages for big brands), not UAE scam ads, so this measures the link layer only. The AI reading of ad text and screenshots is not part of this number. Phishing pages die fast; links already down can only be judged by name, age and hosting. The legitimate set was chosen by hand. Rules were written before this run and were not tuned on it.
